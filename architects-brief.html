Start with the space, not the object.

A bespoke glass or washi artwork should belong to the architecture. Five early decisions make the conversation with a fabricating art studio more precise—and the result easier to evaluate.

atelier TECNOFORM has been making spatial art in Kobe since 1983. This note outlines what is helpful to share before requesting a proposal, whether the project is in Japan or abroad.

Build a project brief
Five things to define

The brief, in practical terms.

01 / Place

Where will it live?

Project type, country and space: lobby, guest room, entrance, feature wall or gallery. A simple location sketch and approximate height, width and viewing distance are useful at the start. Confidential drawings are not required for an initial enquiry.

02 / Light

What changes through the day?

Describe natural light, intended lighting effect, reflections, window exposure and how visitors approach the piece. In glass and translucent washi, the setting changes the artwork.

03 / Material & care

Who will look after it?

Discuss cleaning access, moisture, touch, maintenance and realistic material preferences. Fire, electrical, fixing, building-code and load-bearing questions must be assessed by appropriately qualified project professionals before installation.

04 / Programme

When is a decision needed?

Share the desired opening date and project milestones, even if tentative. Sampling, fabrication, shipping and site installation are distinct stages; overseas logistics and local trade responsibilities are assessed case by case.

05 / Approval

What will make the proposal usable?

Name the design decision maker, the level of visual study needed and an approximate budget range if known. Design fees, physical samples, usage permissions, production and installation are scoped separately; a first enquiry is not an order.

Keep the first message simple

One page is enough to begin.

Space type and city; rough dimensions; atmosphere and material references; desired schedule; who will approve the concept. If any of these are unknown, mark them as undecided instead of inventing precision.

No purchase, quotation or reserved production slot is implied by contacting the studio. The design, rights, technical review and commercial scope are agreed for each project.
