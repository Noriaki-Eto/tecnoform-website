For architects, developers and design teams

From first discussion to an installed work.

We welcome enquiries from international hospitality, workplace, residential and cultural-space teams. We can discuss projects remotely at concept stage. Shipping, local installation, technical approval, programme and commercial terms depend on the location and must be agreed for each project.

Share the project type, location, approximate dimensions, desired timeline and design intent.
